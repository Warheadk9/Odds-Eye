About Arbitrage & Setup

🎯 What is Arbitrage?

Arbitrage is exploiting odds differences between bookmakers to lock in a guaranteed profit, regardless of the outcome. You place calculated bets on all possible outcomes at different bookies where the odds create a profit opportunity.

Example: Bet GHS 100 on Team A winning at Bet365 (odds 2.5), and bet GHS 140 on Team B at Betway (odds 1.8). No matter who wins, you profit GHS 50.

⚠️ Important Risks

  • ✋ Account Bans: Bookies ban accounts that consistently use arbitrage
  • 📊 Odds Changes: Odds shift before you place the second bet, reducing profit
  • 💰 Bet Limits: Winning accounts face lower max bet limits
  • 🚫 Withdrawals: Some bookies restrict withdrawals if they suspect arbitrage
  • ⏱️ Speed Required: You must act fast - opportunities close quickly

  • 💼 Use multiple accounts (family members) to reduce suspicion
  • ⏰ Don't always place bets immediately - space them out
